How Procura works

From a plain language request to an executed, fully traceable purchase.

1

Tell Procura what you need

Describe it in natural language or build a structured request.

2

Let it source and compare

Agents discover suppliers, collect quotes and check the evidence.

3

Review the recommendation

See the best eligible offer, the reasoning and the supporting evidence.

4

Authorise and execute

Approve a spending session with a passkey, the agent pays within your limits.

Give your agent responsibility, not unlimited authority.

Procura can act, but never beyond the financial authority you approve. Every payment is bounded by programmable budgets, per transaction limits and time boxed spending sessions, all enforced by Kite Passport.

%WALLET%

Programmable budgets

Set monthly and per purchase ceilings the agent can never exceed.

%CLOCK%
